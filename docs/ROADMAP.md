`VF-ATTACHMENT-CONTEXT-2026-09-14 — Model-aware attachment context & guard provenance (partially remediated 2026-09-14; extraction chunking deferred).` Verified against live traffic logs and current `main`: (1) all Venice model records now pass through one canonical normalizer (`normalizeModelInfo` in `src/services/modelClassification.ts`) at every ingestion choke point (`mergeCanonicalModels`/`replaceCanonicalModels` in `modelCatalogCache.ts`, the `useModels` select, `flattenModels`), mapping `model_spec.availableContextTokens`, `context_length`, and camelCase variants into `ModelInfo.contextLength`/`maxOutputTokens` — fixing the silent 8,192-token fallback in the chat budget engine and the picker/context-meter disagreement.
